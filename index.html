Welcome to naturo Life Private Limited

Your Trusted Partner in Global Trade


At naturo Life, we are dedicated to providing the finest products from India to the world. Specializing in the export of eco-friendly bamboo toothbrushes, premium-quality onions grown in the renowned Lasalgaon region, and other farm produces we are committed to sustainability and excellence.

WE OFFER

1. Export of Eco-Friendly Bamboo Toothbrushes

We specialize in exporting high-quality, biodegradable bamboo toothbrushes that are designed to promote sustainable living. Our services include:

Custom Branding: We offer private labeling and custom branding options to help businesses create their unique identity in the market.

Bulk Orders: Whether you need a small batch or a large shipment, we cater to orders of all sizes with flexible shipping options.

Quality Assurance: Our toothbrushes are rigorously tested to ensure they meet international quality standards.

2. Export of Premium Lasalgaon Onions

We bring the finest onions from the renowned Lasalgaon region to the world. Our services include:

Quality Selection: We handpick the best onions, known for their rich flavor and long shelf life, to ensure top-notch quality in every shipment.

Efficient Logistics: Our well-established supply chain ensures that the onions are delivered fresh and on time to markets across the globe.

Custom Packaging: We provide tailored packaging solutions to meet the specific requirements of our clients, ensuring the products arrive in perfect condition.

About Us

Founded in 2021 by Prakash Junagade and Vijay Sonawne, Naturo is a company built on the principles of
quality, sustainability, and innovation. With a vision to bring the finest products of India to the global market, Naturo specializes in exporting eco-friendly bamboo toothbrushes and premium onions from the
renowned Lasalgaon region.

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized export company known for quality, sustainability, and ethical sourcing.

Our Mission

To provide premium, eco-friendly Indian products to the world while empowering local farmers and artisans.
